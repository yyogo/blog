Let me back into the land with no names  
Where the lines blur and colors blend  
Where eternity lasts barely a blink  
A dreamless, formless slumber  
May the darkness embrace me  
Cradle me in gentle coils  
Whisper sweet silence  
Shape my thoughts  
Take my words  
Allow me to  
Finally  
Become sleep.  
Rest, return, release  
Put the soul back on the shelf  
Let consciousness fade into being  
Being everything  
Being nothing  
No memory  
No shame  
No name  
Just...
